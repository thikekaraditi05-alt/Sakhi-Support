Frequently Asked Questions

Do mentors get paid?

Currently, mentoring is voluntary. As Sakhi Support grows, premium mentorship opportunities may include honorariums.

Can I mentor remotely?

Yes. All mentorship sessions are conducted online, making it convenient for both mentors and students.

How long is one mentorship session?

A standard mentorship session lasts between 45 and 60 minutes.

How many sessions do I need to conduct?

We recommend at least two sessions per month, but you can choose your own availability.
